Who We Are

About JCXPRESS

JCXPRESS is your trusted transportation service — because every visit matters. We connect families with their incarcerated loved ones across New York State, knowing that maintaining these important relationships shouldn't be limited by distance or transportation barriers.

Keeping families connected one visit at a time. Our experienced team provides safe, reliable transportation every weekend to 12 correctional facilities throughout New York, supporting families during some of life's most challenging times.

Transportation Disclaimer

JCXPRESS operates as a third-party booking and coordination service connecting customers with independent transportation providers. We do not own, operate, or directly provide transportation vehicles or driving services.

All transportation is arranged through licensed, independent operators. JCXPRESS is not liable for any incidents, delays, accidents, or injuries that occur during transportation.

  • JCXPRESS serves as a booking coordinator only
  • Transportation is provided by independent third-party companies
  • Claims or disputes should be directed to the transportation provider
  • JCXPRESS' role does not extend to vehicle operation
02

SMS Messaging Terms

By checking the SMS consent checkbox during booking, you agree to receive text messages from JCXPRESS about your booking confirmations, status updates, and trip notifications.

  • Booking confirmation with pickup location and time
  • Reminder messages before your scheduled trip
  • Important updates and notifications
Frequency: Typically 1–3 messages per booking
Opt-Out: Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe
Help: Reply HELP or call (917) 244-5352
Rates: Message & data rates may apply
03

Booking Terms

All bookings are subject to availability and admin confirmation. Your booking is not finalized until you receive an SMS confirmation from our team.

Confirmation: Required before your trip is finalized
Cancellations: Contact (917) 244-5352 to cancel or modify
Refund Policy: Deposits are only refundable if cancelled within 48 hours of booking. Cancellations after 48 hours are non-refundable.
No-Show: Failure to arrive on time may forfeit your reservation
04

Privacy Policy

We collect your name, phone number, email address, and booking details solely to provide our transportation coordination services.

  • Process and confirm your booking
  • Send SMS and email notifications about your trip
  • Communicate important updates or changes
  • Improve our services
Storage: Securely stored in our database
Sharing: We never sell or trade your personal information
